## Detailed Description

Tired of deleting old AI chats one by one? 

**ChatSweep** is the universal, open-source conversation manager for power users of AI assistants. With one consistent, beautiful interface, ChatSweep lets you search, filter, bulk-select, and safely delete conversation histories across 9 major AI chat platforms:

✨ **Supported Platforms:**
• **Claude** (claude.ai)
• **ChatGPT** (chatgpt.com & chat.openai.com)
• **Google Gemini** (gemini.google.com)
• **Perplexity** (perplexity.ai)
• **DeepSeek** (chat.deepseek.com)
• **Grok** (grok.com & x.com/i/grok)
• **Microsoft Copilot** (copilot.microsoft.com)
• **Mistral Le Chat** (chat.mistral.ai)
• **Poe** (poe.com)

### 🛡️ Why Choose ChatSweep?

🔒 **100% Private & Local Processing**
ChatSweep executes entirely inside your browser. No third-party servers, zero analytics, zero telemetry, and zero tracking. Your conversations and credentials never leave your device.

🎯 **Safe UI Automation**
Instead of using undocumented private APIs that could risk your account, ChatSweep drives the site’s own deletion UI with humanized delays, micro-jitter, and bounded retries.

🛡️ **Multiple Safety Guardrails**
• **Pre-Action Verification:** Checks chat title matching immediately before deleting to prevent mis-clicks or list re-ordering mistakes.
• **Explicit Confirmation:** For batches of 10 or more chats, requires typing "DELETE" to prevent accidental data loss.
• **Dry-Run Mode:** Simulate and test your cleanup before making any real deletions.
• **Pre-Deletion Backup:** One-click export of chat titles and dates to CSV or JSON before cleaning.

⚡ **Powerful Selection & Filters**
• Shift-Click Range Selection: Select hundreds of conversations in seconds.
• Filters: Today, last 7 days, last 30 days, older than X days, or custom date ranges.
• Keyword Search & Exclusion: Keep important projects safe while purging scratch queries.
• Exclude Pinned/Starred Chats by default.
• "Load Full History" auto-scroll helper.

### 📋 Privacy & Permissions Justification

• **storage:** Used exclusively to store your preferred delays, safety thresholds, and local operational activity logs on your computer.
• **host_permissions:** Restricted strictly to the supported AI websites so ChatSweep can display the bulk selection toolbar when you visit them.
